About us

A construction company built on quality workmanship, professionalism and reliable service.

Strong buildings. Sustainable infrastructure.

Gung Construction Limited is based in Kumbungu. We provide reliable, professional construction for individuals, businesses and organizations.

We deliver projects with attention to quality, safety, durability and customer satisfaction, and we want to contribute to buildings and infrastructure that serve communities for years.

Every project deserves proper planning, quality materials, skilled workmanship and careful supervision, and that is how we work.

Our vision

To become a trusted and respected construction company known for quality workmanship, professionalism and reliable service.

Our mission

To provide quality construction services that meet our clients' needs while maintaining high standards of professionalism, safety, efficiency and integrity.
